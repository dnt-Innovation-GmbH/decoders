The dnt® LoRaWAN® radiator thermostat controllable (dnt-lw-eTRV-C) enables direct temperature control with integrated buttons, and shows relevant temperature settings on an e-paper display. Other features are customizable heating profiles, a window open detection and the posibilitiy to perform a dynamic adaptive adjustment of the radiator.Because of very low energy consumption, a battery life up to 3 years can be archived.
