The dnt® LoRaWAN® Radiator Thermostat Controllable (dnt-lw-eTRV-C) enables direct temperature control via integrated buttons and displays relevant temperature settings on an e-paper display. Additional features include customizable heating profiles, window-open detection, and the ability to perform dynamic adaptive adjustment of the radiator. Thanks to its very low energy consumption, a battery life of up to 3 years can be achieved.
