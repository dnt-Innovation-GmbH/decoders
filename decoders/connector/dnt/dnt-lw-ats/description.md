The dnt LoRaWAN® Asset Tracker Solar (dnt-lw-ats) is a reliable and compact GNSS tracker with a water- and dust-proof housing compliant with IP44. Thanks to the built-in solar cell, self-sufficient operation is possible. Position determination can be performed either cyclically or based on motion. Additionally, there are many configuration options available via LoRaWAN® downlinks, such as motion sensitivity and positioning interval. The average sleep mode current is below 10 µA.
